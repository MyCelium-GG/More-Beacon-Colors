# More Beacon Colors

Any color for your beacon beam, picked from a menu in the beacon. Solid colors, gradients, rainbows, color cycles, pulses, rising stripes and drifting auroras, with a live preview. Saved with the world and shown to every player with the mod.

**New in 1.2.0:** candy cane stripes, northern-lights auroras, one-click presets and copy and paste between beacons.

![Three beacons with rainbow, gold and blue gradient beams against the sky](https://wiki.mycelium-gg.org/morebeaconcolors/img/beams.png)

- Solid: the 16 dye colors, 8 extras, or any color from the picker or a hex code
- Gradient: fade through 2 to 16 colors from the bottom of the beam to the top
- Rainbow: stripes that rise up the beam
- Color cycle: the whole beam runs through every color
- Pulse: the beam fades through 2 to 16 colors and back
- Stripes: bands of your colors rising up the beam. Red and white is a candy cane
- Aurora: your colors drift along the beam in slow waves, like northern lights
- Presets: Candy Cane, Christmas Lights, Aurora, Frost, Fire, Ocean, Sunset and Pride, one click each
- Copy and paste: copy a beam and paste it on another beacon, or share its code in chat

![A candy cane beacon beam, red and white stripes rising into the night sky, between rainbow and blue beams](https://wiki.mycelium-gg.org/morebeaconcolors/img/candy-cane.png?v=120)

![The color menu with the Presets list open: Candy Cane, Christmas Lights, Aurora, Frost, Fire, Ocean, Sunset, Pride, then Copy and Paste](https://wiki.mycelium-gg.org/morebeaconcolors/img/presets.png?v=120)
- Slow, normal and fast speeds
- Live preview with day and night sky; scroll to zoom out over the whole beam

![The color menu with a gradient selected and a live beam preview](https://wiki.mycelium-gg.org/morebeaconcolors/img/menu-gradient.png)

## Install

Install it on both sides to share colors. Players without the mod see vanilla beams and can join as usual. On a server without the mod, your colors stay on your computer.

Requires [Mycel](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/mycel): install it alongside this mod. Without it the game still starts, and the mod tells you in chat that Mycel is missing. Minecraft 26.1, 26.2 and 26.3 on Fabric, Forge and NeoForge, Java 25. On Fabric you also need Fabric API.

Every setting and command is on the [wiki](https://wiki.mycelium-gg.org/morebeaconcolors/).
