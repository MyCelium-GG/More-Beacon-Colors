# More Beacon Colors

Any color for your beacon beam, picked from a menu in the beacon. Solid
colors, gradients through as many colors as you like, rainbows, color
cycles and pulses, with a live preview. Saved with the world and shown to
every player with the mod.

- **Button on the beacon screen** opens the color menu
- **Solid**: the 16 dye colors, 8 extras, or any color from the picker or a
  hex code
- **Gradient**: fade through 2 to 16 colors from the bottom of the beam to
  the top
- **Rainbow**: stripes that rise up the beam
- **Color cycle**: the whole beam runs through every color
- **Pulse**: the beam fades through 2 to 16 colors and back
- Slow, normal and fast speeds
- **Live preview** with day and night sky; scroll to zoom out over the
  whole beam
- On a server with the mod, everyone with the mod sees your colors; on a
  server without it, they are kept on your computer
- Players without the mod see vanilla beams and can join as usual
- Config: `enabled`, `showButton`, `allowAnimated`
- Requires **Mycel**: install it alongside this mod

Reload live with `/mycel reload morebeaconcolors`.
Docs: https://wiki.mycelium-gg.org/morebeaconcolors/
