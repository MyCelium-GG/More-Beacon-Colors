# More Beacon Colors

Any color for your beacon beam, picked from a menu in the beacon. Solid colors, gradients, rainbows, color cycles and pulses, with a live preview. Saved with the world and shown to every player with the mod.

![Three beacons with rainbow, gold and blue gradient beams against the sky](https://wiki.mycelium-gg.org/morebeaconcolors/img/beams.png)

- Solid: the 16 dye colors, 8 extras, or any color from the picker or a hex code
- Gradient: fade through 2 to 16 colors from the bottom of the beam to the top
- Rainbow: stripes that rise up the beam
- Color cycle: the whole beam runs through every color
- Pulse: the beam fades through 2 to 16 colors and back
- Slow, normal and fast speeds
- Live preview with day and night sky; scroll to zoom out over the whole beam

![The color menu with a gradient selected and a live beam preview](https://wiki.mycelium-gg.org/morebeaconcolors/img/menu-gradient.png)

## Install

Install it on both sides to share colors. Players without the mod see vanilla beams and can join as usual. On a server without the mod, your colors stay on your computer.

Requires [Mycel](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/mycel): install it alongside this mod. Minecraft 26.1, 26.2 and 26.3 on Fabric, Forge and NeoForge, Java 25. On Fabric you also need Fabric API.

Every setting and command is on the [wiki](https://wiki.mycelium-gg.org/morebeaconcolors/).
