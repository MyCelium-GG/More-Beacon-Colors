# More Beacon Colors (Forge)

Any color for your beacon beam, from a menu in the beacon: solid colors,
gradients, rainbows, color cycles and pulses, with a live preview.

- Dye colors, a color picker and hex codes; gradients through up to 16 colors
- Saved with the world and shown to every player with the mod
- Works on servers without the mod too (only you see the colors)

Requires **Mycel**. Needs Forge, Minecraft 26.3, 26.2 or 26.1 (26.1-26.1.2), Java 25.
